# Comparative Extraction of Anogeissus leiocarpus and its In Vitro Trypanocidal Activities

## 🔬 Project Overview
This independent research project evaluated the therapeutic efficacy of aqueous and organic leaf extracts of *Anogeissus leiocarpus* (African Birch) against *Trypanosoma* species, the causative agents of Animal African Trypanosomiasis (AAT). The laboratory protocols and experimental assays for this study focused on testing botanical alternatives to standard trypanocides.

## 🎯 Core Research Objectives
* To perform comparative extractions of *Anogeissus leiocarpus* utilizing different solvent systems (aqueous vs. organic).
* To evaluate the *in vitro* parasite motility inhibition profiles of the resulting botanical extracts.
* To manage, structure, and analyze baseline experimental screening datasets to determine optimal therapeutic concentration curves.

## 🧪 Technical & Laboratory Competencies Demonstrated
* **Botanical Processing:** Hands-on execution of organic and aqueous plant extraction methodologies.
* **Microscopic Screening:** Advanced microscopic observation and diagnostic tracking of live *Trypanosoma* parasites.
* **In Vitro Assays:** Executing controlled motility inhibition assays under strict laboratory biosafety and biosecurity standards.
* **Data Logging:** Manual collection, documentation, and structural formatting of raw laboratory performance metrics.
